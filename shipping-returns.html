Shipping & returns

We keep fulfillment straightforward and try to make every order feel personal.

Shipping

  • Orders are packed within 3–5 business days.
  • Standard domestic delivery usually takes 2–4 business days after dispatch.
  • You will receive an email when your order leaves our studio.
  • Orders over $75 ship free within the contiguous United States.

Returns

Unused items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Please keep the original packaging when possible.

  1. Contact us with your order number.
  2. We will share the return address and simple packing instructions.
  3. Once received, we will refund the item price to the original payment method.

Items damaged in transit are covered separately. Send us a photo within seven days so we can make it right.
