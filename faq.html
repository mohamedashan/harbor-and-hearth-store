Orders

Do you offer gift notes?

Yes. Add your note to the delivery notes field at checkout and we will write it by hand.

Materials and care

Are your products dishwasher safe?

Care varies by product. The product page always lists the recommended care instructions.

Why does my handmade item look different?

Small variations in shape, color, and texture are natural signs that an item was made by hand.
