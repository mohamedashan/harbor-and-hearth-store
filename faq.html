Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers before you place an order.

Orders

Can I change my order?

Send us a message as soon as possible. If your order has not been packed, we will do our best to update it.

Do you offer gift notes?

Yes. Add your note to the delivery notes field at checkout and we will write it by hand.

How do I track my order?

We send a dispatch email when your order leaves the studio. If you have not received it after five business days, contact us with your order number.

Materials and care

Are your products dishwasher safe?

Care varies by product. The product page always lists the recommended care instructions.

Why does my handmade item look different?

Small variations in shape, color, and texture are natural signs that an item was made by hand.
