Things worth keeping

Harbor & Hearth began with a simple question: what if online shopping felt more like finding something at a neighbor's kitchen table?

Why we choose slowly

We look for materials that feel good in the hand, makers who care about their process, and objects that become more familiar with use. We keep our collection small so every piece earns its place.

Our working values

  1. Useful first. Beauty should make daily work feel lighter.
  2. Honest materials. We tell you what things are made of and how to care for them.
  3. Longer lives. We design for repair, reuse, and passing along.
